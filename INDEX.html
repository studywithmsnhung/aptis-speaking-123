Form: Miêu tả 1 bức tranh

Cấu trúc: Opening – Body (people / scene) – Closing

1. Mở bài (Opening)
  • The picture shows a person who is V-ing O. (số ít)
  • The picture shows people who are V-ing O. (số nhiều)
  • In this picture, I can see . (câu mở thay thế)
2. Thân bài (Body)

Tả người

  • There are people in the picture.
  • S/he looks young / old / middle-aged. Maybe he/she is in his/her (twenties, thirties...).
  • S/he is wearing casual / formal clothes. They look nice on him/her.
  • He/she is V-ing (hành động).
  • Judging from his/her facial expression, I suppose he/she is feeling (cảm xúc).
Tip
"clothes" luôn ở số nhiều → dùng "They look nice", không dùng "It looks nice". Nói tuổi: "in his/her twenties/thirties", không phải "at his/her...".

Tả khung cảnh

  • Overall, the picture looks nice and lively.
  • In the background, I can see many things, such as .
  • I guess the weather is hot / cold / pleasant.
Tip
Lỗi hay gặp: "guest" (khách) ≠ "guess" (đoán). Dùng "I guess..." cho phần phỏng đoán thời tiết.
3. Kết bài (Closing)

Một câu dùng chung cho mọi bức tranh:

  • All in all, it is a pleasant picture, and it reminds me of / makes me feel .
  • All in all, I think it is a lovely picture that I really enjoy looking at. (bản ngắn gọn hơn)

Form: So sánh 2 bức tranh

Cấu trúc: Opening – Similarities / Differences – Closing

1. Mở bài (Opening)

The two pictures show people (relaxing / studying / working / eating) in different places.

2. Thân bài (Body)
Điểm giống (Similarities)
  • Both pictures share several things in common.
  • Both look lively with gorgeous colours.
  • The people in both pictures seem to be (cảm xúc).
Tip: "Both" đi với động từ số nhiều → "Both look", không phải "Both looks".
Điểm khác (Differences)

There are still some clear differences between the two.

1. Số lượng / tuổi người

First of all, let's talk about the number and age of the people.

In the first picture, there are whereas in the second picture, there are (giới tính, tuổi tác...)

2. Nơi chốn

Secondly, the two pictures were taken in different places.

I suppose the first one was taken in and the second was taken in

3. Hành động

The actions of the people are also completely different.

The first picture shows people V-ing but the second shows people V-ing.

Tip
Sửa lỗi hay gặp: "a bout" → "about"; "clearly differences" → "clear differences"; "The actions ... is" → "are".
3. Kết bài (Closing)

Một câu dùng chung cho mọi bài so sánh:

All in all, despite a few differences, both pictures share the same lively atmosphere, and I really enjoy looking at them.
